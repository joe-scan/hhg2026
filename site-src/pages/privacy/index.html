Privacy

Last changed 20 September 2026. The first ten games are being taken by email and built by hand, so this covers both the free demo, which sends us nothing, and what an order involves.

HappyHeroGames makes a game about a child, so the rule we work to is simple: collect as little as possible, show less, and delete it when you ask. This page says exactly what that means. If anything here is unclear, email hello@happyherogames.com and we'll answer in plain English.

The free demo sends us nothing

The name, hair, colors, family member and pet you choose on the front page never leave your browser. They travel to the game inside the web address itself, and a copy is saved on your own device so your work is still there if you come back. Nothing is sent to us, and there is no account to make.

To clear that copy, clear your browser's site data for happyherogames.com.

What we'll collect when you order

For an order we ask for what the game needs and nothing else:

  • First names, or what people are called at home: Mom, Grandma, Coach.
  • What the hero loves doing, the family's catchphrases and running jokes, favorite foods.
  • The pet's name, kind and color.
  • The occasion, and the date you need it by.
  • Your own email address and payment details, so we can send the game and take the money.

What we never collect

  • Photographs. The hero is drawn in pixels from choices you make.
  • Voice recordings. We tried the idea and dropped it.
  • Surnames, ages, dates of birth, schools, clubs, addresses or anything about where a child goes.
  • The pet's breed, which is a security question answer on half the internet.

Payment

Card details go to our payment processor, never to us. We see that an order was paid for, the email address and the country, which is what the tax rules need.

Who else sees it

A person here reads and plays every game before it's sent, which is how the jokes stay kind. Beyond that: the payment processor, the company that hosts the site, and the printer who makes the poster for a gift box order. The printer gets a delivery address and a picture to print, and nothing about the child.

We don't sell anything to anybody, and we don't run advertising trackers on the game.

Where the games live

Every game has its own web address that can't be guessed, and search engines are told not to list it. Anyone with that address can play the game, so treat it like a photo of your family: fine to send to Granny, not for posting in public. We can move a game to a new address, or take it down, whenever you ask.

Visitor statistics

The front page counts visits using Statcounter, which sets a cookie and records the page, the browser and a rough location. It's on the front page only. The game pages have no analytics at all, because their web addresses contain a family's details and those are nobody else's business.

How long we keep things

Your game stays up, and your answers stay on file, for as long as you want the game to keep working. Ask us to delete them and we'll delete them, and the game stops working at that point. We delete unfinished orders after ninety days. Records we have to keep for tax, such as what was paid and when, we keep for six years.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of everything we hold, ask us to fix it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@happyherogames.com and we'll do it within thirty days, usually the same week. If you're in the EU or the UK and you're not happy with how we handled it, you can complain to your data protection authority. In Ireland that's the Data Protection Commission.

Children

The buyer is an adult: a parent, grandparent or friend of the family. We ask them to confirm they're allowed to share the details they're giving us about the child and everyone else in the game. Children don't have accounts here and can't give us anything themselves.
