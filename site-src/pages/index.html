Questions

What device do I need?

Any phone, tablet, laptop or computer with a web browser.

How quickly can I get it?

The same day you pay. We email you their link, and it works straight away.

How do I give it to them?

We email you a web address that is theirs alone. Send it on the day by message or email, or write it inside a card. Add a poster or a t-shirt with their hero on it and it arrives in the post, so there is something to wrap.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. Type their name and the game is already on the screen. Everything else is optional.

Who can be in the game?

Up to eight people: family, friends, a teacher, a coach. The pet too: a dog, cat, rabbit, hamster or fish.

How long does it take to play?

Ten to twenty minutes the first time through, depending on how much they replay the ones they lose.

Can a few children play together?

Yes. Party mode is on the first screen. Pick how many are playing, and each one takes a turn against the hero. It ends on a results board with a champion.

What if something is wrong when it arrives?

Tell us what is wrong and we will fix it and send a new link. There is no charge for that.
