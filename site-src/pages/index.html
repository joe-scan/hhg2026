Questions

What does it play on?

Any phone, tablet or laptop with a browser. Nothing to install. Two people can play on one keyboard, or share a phone turned sideways.

Who can be in it?

Up to eight people: family, friends, a teacher, a coach. And the pet, which can be a dog, cat, rabbit, hamster or fish.

Can a whole party play?

Yes. Party mode on the title screen: pick how many challengers, and each one takes a turn against the hero while everyone else watches. It ends on a results board with a champion.

Can I change something after it arrives?

Tell us what's wrong and we'll fix it.
