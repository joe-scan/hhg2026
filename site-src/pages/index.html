Questions

What device do I need?

Any phone, tablet, laptop or computer with a web browser. There is nothing to download and nothing to install. Two people can play on one keyboard, or share a phone held sideways.

How do I give it to them?

We email you a web address that is theirs alone. Send it on the day by message or email, or write it inside a card. The gift box also has a printed poster, which arrives in the post so there is something to wrap.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. You answer a few questions in a form, the way you would fill in an address. We build the game and send you the link.

Who can be in the game?

Up to eight people: family, friends, a teacher, a coach. The pet too: a dog, cat, rabbit, hamster or fish.

How long does it take to play?

About twenty minutes the first time through. Then they play it again, and hand it to whoever is in the room.

Can a few children play together?

Yes. Party mode is on the first screen. Pick how many are playing. Each one takes a turn against the hero while the rest watch. It ends on a results board with a champion.

What if something is wrong when it arrives?

Tell us what is wrong and we will fix it and send a new link. There is no charge for that.
