What you're buying

A web game made for one person, at its own web address, playable in any modern browser. $39 buys one game at a link of its own. $99 buys all five games and the ending. A game bought at $39 can be upgraded to all five for $60 at the same address. Printed things with the hero on them, such as a postcard, a t-shirt, a poster or a mug, are priced separately and only added if you say yes to a proof.

You're buying a made-to-order thing, not a licence to something we already had. We keep the right to reuse the underlying games and code; you get your family's version of them.

When it arrives

The game's link is emailed to you the same day you pay. Printed things are made to order and posted to you; we'll give you a delivery estimate before you pay for them.

If something's wrong

Tell us and we'll fix it. A spelling, a name, a joke that lands badly, anything we got wrong: we'll change it and send a new link, once, at no charge. After that we'll still help, we just may ask for a bit of time.

Refunds

  • Within seven days of paying: a full refund for the game, for any reason. Just ask.
  • After seven days, if it's not what we described or it doesn't work: a full refund.
  • Something printed that arrives damaged or misprinted: we reprint it. Send a photo.

What we need from you

That you're an adult, that the details you give us about a child and about anyone else in the game are yours to share, and that what you write is kind. We read every game before it ships and we won't put anything cruel in one, even as a joke. If we can't build what you've asked for, we'll say so and refund you.

Taking a game down

Ask us to take your game down and we'll take it down. If we ever stop trading, we'll give you a copy you can keep and run yourself.

Things we can't be responsible for

A browser that's ten years old, a lost link you never told us about, or the emotional consequences of a granny being beaten at a water balloon fight. Our liability is limited to what you paid.

Who you're dealing with
