# Deutsch: please check this translation

Everything HappyHeroGames says in Deutsch, beside the English it came from. It was translated by an AI and has never been read by a native speaker, which is why you are looking at it.

**How to help:** change anything in the right-hand column. Leave the left column alone. Send the file back and it goes straight into the site.

Two things to know. The text inside the game is drawn in a pixel font that has no capital É, Í, Ó, Ö, À or Ç, so those are written without the accent on purpose. Á, Ñ and Ü are fine. And a line that starts or ends with a space joins onto a name, so "GANO " becomes "GANO AVA".

The jokes matter more than the legal text. If the taunts do not sound like something a real German-speaking family would say, rewrite them completely.
