# Fifty ways this could spread

Written 20 September 2026. Ranked inside each group by what it costs us against what it could return. Effort is Claude days unless it says otherwise: **S** is under a day, **M** two to three days, **L** a week or more.

## The rule about real people

No real person goes in a game. Not a president, not a footballer, not a pop star. Three reasons, and the first one is enough.

1. We sell to parents. A game with a politician in it gets us thrown out of the school WhatsApp group, whichever politician it is.
2. Names, faces and catchphrases are owned. Parody is a defence you have to pay a solicitor to run.
3. The joke ages in a week and the brand wears it for years.

The topical idea still works, though. Take the *situation*, not the person: an election-night vote-counting scramble, a heatwave water fight, a tariff-driven trolley dash where the prices keep going up. Everyone gets the joke, nobody gets a letter.

## 1. Free seasonal games, each with one shareable picture (the engine)

The five to build first. Each is a small free game on the site, playable in a minute, ending on one image with the player's name on it and a "make one for your family" link.

1. **Trick or Treat Dash** (built 20 Sep 2026, at `/free/halloween/`). Catch the sweets, leave the sprouts, sixty seconds. It ends on their name in lights with their score, ready to send.
2. **Sleigh Dash** (built 20 Sep 2026, at `/free/christmas/`). One button: run the rooftops, and a present goes down every chimney you clear. A fall costs three seconds, never the run.
3. **Egg Hunt** (Easter, S). Twenty eggs, sixty seconds, a family scoreboard.
4. **Sports Final Free-Taker** (whenever a big final is on, S). Penalties against a goalkeeper with your own name on the jersey. No club, no crest, no player.
5. **Back to School Sprint** (late August, S). Grab the books, dodge the socks. Parents share this one because it is exactly how the morning feels.

The point of all five is the same: free to play, no data, thirty seconds to a picture worth sending.

## What I would actually do first

Party mode, the name in lights generator, Trick or Treat Dash and Sleigh Dash are all built, and they live together at `/free/`. Next: put the Halloween game in front of people in the first week of October and the Christmas one in late November, then see whether anybody sends them on. If they do, build Easter. If they do not, the rest of this list is guesswork and the effort belongs in the paid product instead.
