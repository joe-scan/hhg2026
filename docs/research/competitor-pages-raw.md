❤️ How exactly does all of this work?

First, you select the game you want and personalise it to your liking by writing custom content (like messages or questions) and creating the characters that will be in the game. Then, after purchasing, you will receive an email with a unique, secret link to play your personalised game. Finally, you can give your gift by copy and pasting the link and sending it to your intended recipient. All they have to do is click on the link and they can play your game directly in their browser on their mobile or desktop device\!

Check out our simple step guide here\!

❤️ How long will my personalised game take to be ready to play?

Your game will be ready to play instantly\! After completing your purchase, you should receive an email in a few moments that contains your unique link to play your game.

❤️ How can my personalised game be played?

After purchasing your game, you should receive an email containing a summary of your order plus your unique secret play link. It should look something like this:

https://minigamegift.com/play/XXXXX

Just open that link in your browser to play your game, no downloads required.

❤️ What devices can my personalised game be played on?

All of our games run in your browser. We currently support the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Safari on iOS, Android and desktop devices.

❤️ Is my personalised game private?

When you create your personalised game, you will be send a unique secret link to play your game. Only people that have that link will be able to play it. Who you share it with is up to you\!

❤️ Do I have to download my personalised game to play it?

No. All of our games run in your browser so no downloads are required\!

❤️ How many times can my personalised game be played?

Your game can be played as many times as you want\!

❤️ Can I change elements of my game after I have purchased it?

No. Make sure you're 100% happy with your customisations before purchasing.

❤️ I have made a purchase but haven't received my confirmation email.

First, double check your junk/spam folder. If it's not there then get in contact with us and we will do our best to help you.

❤️ Are my payment details secure on Minigamegift?

Yes. In addition to using SSL/TLS, (check the padlock icon in your browser) we use a company called Stripe to process your payments securely. Your card and address details are handled by Stripe and never actually pass through our servers at all\!

❤️ I still have a question that wasn't answered in the FAQ, can you help?

Of course we can\! Get in contact with us and we will get back to you as soon as possible.

What is GameGift?

GameGift is a platform that allows you to create personalised games for your loved ones. Imagine gifting someone a game that’s been custom-made just for them\! Whether it's a quirky clicker game, a thrilling escape challenge, or an exciting boat shooter, you can now make it personal by customising the game's assets, text, and more.

How It Works

Creating a personalised game with GameGift is super simple:

Choose a Game Template: We currently offer three templates — Clicker, Escape the Punch, and Boat Shoot.

Customise the Assets: Swap out images to tailor the game to the recipient. You can add inside jokes, favourite photos, or anything else that makes the game special.

Share the Game: Once you’re happy with your creation, you can purchase it for just £5 and get a unique URL to share with your recipient.

Why I Built GameGift

We’ve all been there—trying to find a gift that’s both unique and meaningful. Traditional gifts can feel impersonal, especially when they’re store-bought. As someone who loves gaming and creativity, I wanted to build something that allows people to create memorable and fun gifts that have a personal touch.

GameGift was born from the idea that a gift should be more than just an object; it should be an experience. And what better experience than playing a game created just for you?

Tech Stack

For those interested in the tech behind GameGift, here’s a quick overview:

Frontend \+ Backend: Built with React.js \+ Next.js

UI: ShadCN

Database: MongoDB

Pricing: Stripe

Game development: Kaboom.js

Hosting: Netlify

What’s Next?

I’m excited to continue improving GameGift and adding more game templates and customisation options. My goal is to make it the go-to platform for creative and personalised gaming experiences.

I’d love to hear your feedback and ideas. Whether you’re a developer with thoughts on the tech stack, or someone who just loves the idea of personalised games, your input is invaluable\!

Try It Out

Curious to see how it works? You can check out GameGift at https://game-gift.netlify.app. Feel free to create a game, try it out, and let me know what you think\!

Thanks for reading, and I hope you enjoy creating personalised games as much as I’ve enjoyed building this platform\!

Happy coding, and happy gifting\! 🎉
