It started with two brothers

Happy Hero Games began as an arcade game one dad made for his two sons. It was full of their catchphrases, their dog and their fights over the front seat, and it ended with the pair of them beating the bedtime clock together.

Every family has those details. We put them in a game.

Private by design

  • No photos. Your hero is drawn in pixels from the choices you make.

Questions

What does it play on?

Any phone, tablet or laptop with a web browser. Two people can play on one keyboard, or on a phone turned sideways with a set of buttons each.

Can the grown-ups play too?

Yes. In 2-player mode a grown-up takes the other side, so Dad really is playing against them.

We don't have a pet.

Leave it out and someone else in the family referees. You can also pick a cat, a rabbit, a hamster or a fish instead of a dog. The fish stays in its bowl and cheers.

How long does it take to play?

The demo takes about ten minutes. The full game is longer, and they'll want to go again.

Can I change something after it arrives?

Deluxe includes one change. For the others, tell us what's wrong and we'll fix it.
