Terms and refunds

Last changed 20 September 2026.

A draft. A solicitor has not read this yet, and it needs to before any money changes hands.

What you're buying

A web game made for one person, at a private link, playable in any modern browser. The game at $99 has five games and a boss, up to eight people in it, and lines written from the answers you give us. The gift box at $179 adds a printed poster and a short trailer of the game.

You're buying a made-to-order thing, not a licence to something we already had. We keep the right to reuse the underlying games and code; you get your family's version of them, for as long as you want it.

When it arrives

Two working days from the moment we have your answers, unless you ask for a specific date, in which case we deliver on that date. If we can't make your date we'll tell you before taking any money. If we're late, you get it free.

Posters ship separately, printed in your own country where we can. We'll give you a delivery estimate before you pay.

If something's wrong

Tell us and we'll fix it. A spelling, a name, a joke that lands badly, anything we got wrong: we'll change it and send a new link, once, at no charge. After that we'll still help, we just may ask for a bit of time.

Refunds

  • Before we start building: full refund, no questions.
  • After it's delivered, if it's not what we described or it doesn't work: full refund.
  • After it's delivered, and it works, but you've changed your mind: no refund, because it was made for you and can't be sold to anyone else. This is the exception for personalised goods in EU and UK consumer law, and by ordering you agree to it.
  • A printed poster that arrives damaged or misprinted: we reprint it. Send a photo.

What we need from you

That you're an adult, that the details you give us about a child and about anyone else in the game are yours to share, and that what you write is kind. We read every game before it ships and we won't put anything cruel in one, even as a joke. If we can't build what you've asked for, we'll say so and refund you.

Your game staying up

We'll keep your game online for as long as you want it there. If we ever stop trading we'll give you a copy you can keep and run yourself. Ask us to take it down and we'll take it down.

Things we can't be responsible for

A browser that's ten years old, a lost link you never told us about, or the emotional consequences of a granny being beaten at a quiz by an eight-year-old. Our liability is limited to what you paid.

Who you're dealing with

HappyHeroGames, run by Joe Scanlon in Ireland. Irish law applies. Email hello@happyherogames.com and a person will answer.
